# KYRUMA Sales Sprint v1

## Objective

Increase the probability of the first KYRUMA EXPRESS purchase without paid media while preserving KYRUMA's premium positioning.

## Immediate funnel

KYRUMA content / search / outreach → KYRUMA EXPRESS or KYRUMA MATCH → Instagram Reset → Shopify checkout → brief → delivery.

## Conversion changes

- Surface KX-001 price directly in primary navigation.
- Give `/express` a direct checkout CTA instead of forcing every buyer through another page.
- Keep KYRUMA MATCH as the no-risk route for visitors who do not know what they need.
- Add fit criteria, personalized-delivery reassurance and launch bonus explanation.
- Add an explicitly illustrative before/after example; never present it as a client result.
- Add FAQ and repeated purchase CTAs to KX-001.
- Keep KX-002–KX-005 hidden until confirmed live and purchasable.

## Non-negotiable checkout gate

Before scaling traffic, verify that Shopify accepts an immediate consumer-friendly payment method (card / Shopify Payments and/or another configured online provider). Manual bank deposit alone is not an acceptable primary path for a €29 impulse purchase.

## Evidence policy

No fabricated testimonials, client counts, conversion rates, urgency or remaining-slot counters.
